II — PRODUCTWHAT IT ACTUALLY DOES

MEMORY, ORCHESTRATION,
PERMISSIONED ACTION.

Three capabilities, each with a boundary. The system remembers what it was allowed to keep, routes work to specialist seats, and stops at the gate before anything leaves the house.

01 · MEMORY
PERSISTENT, SOURCED
Every record carries its origin. Recall is retrieval, not reconstruction — and retrieval can miss. You can read, export and delete your own history.
02 · ORCHESTRATION
TWENTY SEATS
Specialist agents with declared responsibilities, tools and permissions. One shared context so a seat does not relearn what another already established.
03 · ACTION
PERMISSIONED, LOGGED
Read-only work runs. Reversible work runs and is recorded. Consequential work waits for your approval, and the approval is written down.
04 · CONTINUITY
SURVIVES THE WINDOW
A new session is not a new relationship. Decisions, corrections and unfinished work carry forward as state you can inspect.
05 · DURABILITY
BACKED UP, RESTORED
The memory store is backed up on a schedule, the backup is verified, and a restore is drilled rather than assumed.
06 · AUDIT
A TRAIL A STRANGER CAN FOLLOW
Every consequential action leaves a receipt: what ran, what it touched, what changed, and who approved it.

HOW A SEAT EARNS ITS PERMISSIONS
READ-ONLY
Search, read, summarise, compute. Runs without asking, because nothing changes.
REVERSIBLE
Writes that can be rolled back. Runs and records the before-state so the change can be undone.
CONSEQUENTIAL
Sending, paying, deleting, publishing. Stops. Waits. Writes down your answer.

VI — LIMITSWHAT THIS IS NOT

THE HONEST COLUMN.

STATED PLAINLY, BECAUSE A PRODUCT THAT HIDES THESE IS LYING TO YOU
  • Not a benchmark winner. No claim here rests on beating a named frontier model on a framed task. We have not published that comparison.
  • Memory is retrieval, not omniscience. Records are stored with sources; retrieval can still miss what was stored.
  • Continuity is a durability commitment, not immortality. Your history is exportable and backed up. No system survives its hardware forever.
  • Simulated consciousness is a research programme, labelled as such, not a settled property of the product.
  • You decide access. Not "upload everything" — what it may read, remember, act upon and delete is yours to set, and yours to revoke.
  • Seat capability follows permissions. Integrations, credentials and approvals decide what can actually run.
  • Not financial, legal or medical advice. The system prepares; a qualified human decides.
  • Prices are advertised, not quoted. Entitlements are confirmed in writing before charge.
