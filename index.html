THE MECHANICS — HOW THE LEFT COLUMN UNDERWRITESFORMULAS SO THE RIGHT COLUMN CAN BE CHECKED
BUILDABLE GFA
GFA = site area × FSR (TOD SEPP controls); net saleable ≈ 0.80 × GFA. 1,600m² at 3.5:1 → 5,600m² of floorspace.
Multiply the land size by the zoning number. That is how much building the site allows.
CAP-RATE WRITE-DOWN
Required yield = 10Y (5.4%) + 200bp ≈ 7.4%. A 6.1–6.5% asset re-prices −17.5% to −24% to reach it on flat income.
Every 1% cap rates rise, building values drop ~13–16%. Office maths is broken until prices fall 20%+.
RESIDUAL LAND VALUE
RLV = GDV ÷ (1 + 18% margin) − hard cost − finance carry − statutory. Hard costs $5,400–6,800/m² GFA; mezzanine carry 14–18% p.a.
Take what the finished building sells for, subtract build cost, interest and profit. What is left is the maximum you can pay for the dirt.
DISTRESS HAIRCUT
Haircut = 1 − RLV ÷ peak-2025 valuation. Mortgagee sale triggers above ~75% look-through LVR; receivers clear at 62–72c.
If they bought at the 2025 peak with shadow-bank debt, their equity is gone. Bid to the receiver, not the owner.
